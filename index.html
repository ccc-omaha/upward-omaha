Why Upward

A league that puts kids first

  • Everyone plays

    Every soccer and flag football player is on the field for at least half of every game. Cheerleaders are part of the whole game day.

  • Affordable

    One registration fee covers the season and the uniform kit, with a sibling discount for families.

  • Low commitment

    One weeknight practice and one Saturday game a week. That's it.

  • Safe and supportive

    Trained coaches, balanced teams, and a low-pressure environment where kids can try, fail, and try again.

  • Rooted in faith

    Built on Christian principles, with coaches who model character on and off the field.

  • Real growth

    Kids build skills, confidence, teamwork, and friendships that last past the final whistle.

Teams and requests

How we build balanced teams

During registration, you'll answer a few questions about your child's skill level. Answer as objectively as you can, based on how your child would play in a recreational league, not a club or travel team. It's how we keep teams even and games fun. No system is perfect, and some teams will still win more than others.

Can my child play with a friend?

We'll do our best to honor one friend request per child. Make sure both families submit the same request. Requests can't be honored when the kids are in different divisions, when a division has separate boys and girls teams and the kids are different genders, or when they chose different practice nights. Multiple friend requests for the same child or team aren't allowed.

Can I avoid a certain practice night?

Practice nights are Monday, Tuesday, and Thursday evenings. We'll try to honor one practice-night exclusion per player, but because of the league's size we can't guarantee it. We can't take requests for specific practice times. Siblings may not end up practicing on the same night.

What if my friend request and practice night conflict?

For soccer, the practice-night exclusion wins. For flag football, the friend request wins.

Why is my child in a different division than their friend?

Soccer is organized by age, using a July 31 birthday cutoff, not by grade. An 8-year-old in U9 and a friend who just turned 9 in U11 will land in different divisions. Flag football and cheer are organized by the grade your child is going into, and registration numbers decide how divisions are grouped.

When will I hear about my child's team?

The Upward leadership team sends team placement, practice night and time, team name, coach, and season schedule by text and email before the season starts. See the key dates for each sport.

Can my child switch teams after placement?

No. Once a player is placed on a team, the size of the league means we can't move players to different teams.

Get involved

It takes a team

Upward runs on volunteers. Give an hour a week, every other week, or whenever you can.

  • Coach

    Love Jesus and want to make a difference in kids' lives? You don't need coaching experience, just a willingness to lead. We'll train and equip you. It's one practice and one game a week.

  • Referee (paid and volunteer)

    We pay our referees, and we train them too. Open to adults, college students, and high schoolers. Experience in the sport helps but isn't required. Hours count toward National Honor Society and school service requirements.

  • Game day team

    Set up and break down fields, help MC, and make Saturdays feel like a big deal.

  • Mowing and field care

    Free during the week? Help mow and maintain the CCC sports fields.

  • Communications and admin

    Help with parent communication, scheduling, and behind-the-scenes organization.

  • Food trucks

    Run a food or refreshment truck? Game days draw big crowds that love supporting local businesses. We can't guarantee sales, but we'd love to have you.
